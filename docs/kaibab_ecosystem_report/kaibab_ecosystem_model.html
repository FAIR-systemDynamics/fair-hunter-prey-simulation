Unit consistency

Each equation is evaluated dimensionally from the declared units of its inputs and compared with what the variable itself declares. 42 of 42 variables declare units. 18 are constants, whose declared units are definitional and so are not cross-checked. Unit names are matched case-insensitively and de-pluralised, so Vehicles, vehicles and Vehicle are one unit. Dmnl, Index, share and fraction all count as dimensionless.

No dimensional inconsistencies found.

Anything unknown in a subexpression makes the whole result unknown and is not reported, so this section under-reports rather than guesses. Bare numbers are taken to adopt whatever units they are used with, matching Vensim, so MAX(0, inventory) is not an error.

Self-checks. Eigenvalue elasticities must sum to one for every mode. The largest deviation here was 8.97e-16. Pathway contributions must sum to the change in each stock's net rate. Compared against a difference of the simulated rates the largest gap was 19.9%, and the two agreed at 87% of sampled points. A model with policy switches or kinked tables breaks the finite difference at those points and nowhere else, so the worst case alone says little. The agreement share is the more informative number.
